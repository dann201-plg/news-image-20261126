A one-page newspaper front page summing up Thursday 26 November 2026 in 40 date-verified stories: Trump proposes new tariffs on 14 trade partners, the Bank of Korea raises its rate to 3.25%, the Nikkei rebounds 1.3%, and the Macy's 100th parade moves to Saturday as a nor'easter hits New York on Thanksgiving Day. Hybrid method: text and layout are drawn by PIL (sharp, deterministic); the three hero scenes are AI editorial illustrations saved once as repo assets and composited by the program; the tariff bar chart, the schematic map and the timeline are drawn by the program. Every story was checked against its primary source's own date; the date-check box in the footer names the 25 November stories that were left out.

## Run it

```sh
python3 draw_news26.py
```
